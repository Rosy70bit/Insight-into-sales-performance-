Insight into Sales PerformanceAn end-to-end data analysis project exploring  retail performance, identifying margin drivers, and visualizing sales trends. Built using AWS Cloud Infrastructure, Google Sheets, and Amazon QuickSight .This project analyzes the Superstore dataset to uncover revenue, profit, and discount dynamics across regions, product categories, and seasons. The goal is to provide actionable recommendations for improving profitability and optimizing pricing strategies.Technical Architecture & WorkflowData Ingestion: Extracted the raw Superstore dataset from Kaggle.Cloud Storage: Provisioned an AWS S3 bucket (ngoni-capstone-2026) to host raw and processed datasets securely.Data Processing & Cleaning: Cleaned row IDs, standardized discount formatting, and updated currency values in Google Sheets.Visualization & Dashboards: Built interactive dashboards in Google Sheets and Amazon QuickSight to track multi-variable KPIs.Key Analytical FindingsConsistent Business Growth: Total sales grew from $484,247 (2011) to $733,947 (2014), while annual profit nearly doubled from $49,544 to $93,508.The Discount Dilemma:The Furniture category generated $741,999 in sales but only $18,451 in profit (2.49% margin) due to deep discounting.Sub-categories like Tables (-$17,725) and Bookcases (-$3,472) suffered heavy net losses driven by average discounts above 20%.Regional Dynamics: The West Region led overall performance ($725,457 sales / $108,418 profit) with low average discounting (10.93%). The Central Region suffered margin suppression (7.92%) due to high discount rates (24.04%).Seasonality: Strong end-of-year sales spikes occur predictably during fall and winter (Q4).Strategic RecommendationsRevise Discount Strategy: Move away from high-volume, low-margin discounting on underperforming sub-categories (Tables, Bookcases).Focus on High-Margin Products: Prioritize top performers like the Canon imageCLASS 2200 Copier ($25,199 profit) and high-margin office tech.Prepare for Q4 Surges: Scale inventory, warehouse staffing, and logistics ahead of the Q4 holiday surge.Connect & ContactAuthor: Ngonidzashe Roselyne KazondaGitHub: Rosy70bitLinkedIn: Ngonidzashe Kazonda ProfileEmail: kazondarosy@gmail.com
